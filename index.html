01 — MEN HAQIMDA

O‘zim haqimda

Men Kotlin va Java asosida Android ilovalar ishlab chiqa oladigan Android dasturchiman. Zamonaviy va qulay mobil ilovalar yaratish, yangi texnologiyalarni o‘rganish hamda ularni real loyihalarda qo‘llash — mening asosiy yo‘nalishim.

Loyihalarim davomida Jetpack Compose, MVP, MVVM, MVI, Clean Architecture, Room, Retrofit, Hilt, Coroutines va boshqa Android texnologiyalari bilan ishlaganman va ishlab kelmoqdaman.

Maqsadim — Android development bo‘yicha professional darajaga yetishish va foydalanuvchilar uchun foydali, sifatli hamda ishonchli mobil ilovalar yaratish.
