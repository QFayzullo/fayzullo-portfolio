01 — MEN HAQIMDA

O‘zim haqimda

Men Kotlin va Java asosida Android ilovalar ishlab chiqa oladigan Android dasturchiman. Zamonaviy va qulay mobil ilovalar yaratish, yangi texnologiyalarni o‘rganish hamda ularni real loyihalarda qo'llay olaman

Loyihalarim davomida Jetpack Compose, MVVM, Clean Architecture, Room, Retrofit, Hilt, Coroutines va boshqa Android texnologiyalari bilan ishlaganman va ishlab kelmoqdaman

Maqsadim — Android development bo‘yicha professional darajaga yetishish va foydalanuvchilar uchun foydali, sifatli hamda ishonchli mobil ilovalar yaratish.
